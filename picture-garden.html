01 / THEIR COLLECTIONS

Multiple curated collections.

Give collections names that mean something to your child, from holidays to everyday adventures. Children can choose a collection from a simple list and browse the photos inside.

Picture Garden displays photos from your device without changing the original files. Adding a photo to a collection does not create a copy. Removing a photo from a collection removes it from Picture Garden, not from your device.

02 / FAMILY CONTROLS

Settings for grown-ups.

Access to family settings is protected by your device's PIN or biometric authentication. A one-time purchase unlocks advanced features.

Choose the settings that work for your family before handing your device to your child.

03 / PHOTO VIEWING

Safely view photos

Picture Garden displays photos full screen and can cycle through them as a slideshow. Children can tap or swipe to navigate a collection.

Child Mode helps keep children in Picture Garden while they use your device.
