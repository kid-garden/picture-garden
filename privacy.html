Who we are

Picture Garden is a mobile app for children, provided by Kid Garden (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Privacy contact: privacy@kid-garden.com

Information and how we use it

Kid Garden does not collect user data through Picture Garden. The app does not collect or access personal or sensitive information, including personally identifiable information, financial or payment details, authentication information, contacts, device location, SMS or call data, health or Health Connect data, a list of installed apps, camera or microphone data, or other sensitive device or usage data.

This notice covers the Picture Garden mobile app. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may process technical request information when your browser retrieves them. The artwork is hosted locally on this site.

Sharing, service providers, and advertising

Kid Garden does not sell or share user data collected through Picture Garden. All purchase transactions are handled by Google Play. Kid Garden does not process payments or collect payment card or billing details. Google Play may process information needed to complete a purchase under its own Privacy Policy.

No accounts are created in Picture Garden.

Children and families

Picture Garden is intended for children. The app does not collect personal or sensitive user data and does not create accounts. A parent or guardian with a question can contact Kid Garden using the privacy contact listed above.

Google Play Console audience, Families, advertising, and Data safety declarations should accurately reflect the released app and its SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Kid Garden does not collect or store user data through Picture Garden, and the app does not create accounts. There is no app account or user data deletion process. Google Play handles purchase transactions and manages related information under its own policies and controls.

Security, transfers, and changes

Kid Garden does not store personal or sensitive user data collected through Picture Garden. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google’s policies.

We will update this notice if our data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@kid-garden.com.
