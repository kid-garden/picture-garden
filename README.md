# Kid Garden / Picture Garden

Public website for Kid Garden and its children's mobile app, Picture Garden.

## GitHub Pages

In the repository settings, open **Pages** and select **Deploy from a branch**, then choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)`. The home page is `index.html`; the privacy page is `privacy.html`.

## Before publishing

The privacy page is intentionally marked as incomplete. Do not submit its URL to Google Play or represent it as the final privacy policy until every `[NEEDS OWNER DETAILS]` item has been replaced with verified information about the released app and all SDKs/services it uses.

Confirm and document:

- The responsible legal entity, mailing address, and a monitored privacy contact.
- Every data type collected, accessed, or received, the source, purpose, and legal basis/consent.
- Every SDK and third party, what information it handles, and its privacy disclosures.
- Whether advertising is present and the child-directed safeguards in use.
- Age groups, parent notice/consent, and family privacy controls.
- Retention and deletion rules; if accounts exist, deletion from both inside the app and an external web resource.
- Security safeguards, international data transfers, and how policy changes are communicated.
- Consistency between the final policy, the app, Google Play's target audience/Families declarations, and the Data safety form.

The privacy policy must be publicly accessible, kept current, linked from the app and the relevant Google Play listing, and accurately describe actual behavior. Review Google's current [privacy policy guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en#privacy_policy) and [Families policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?ref_topic=9877467) before release.
